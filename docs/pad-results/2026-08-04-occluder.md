# An infrared-absorbing chin: the attack the relief measurement asked for

The relief measurement ([2026-08-04-landmark-relief.md](2026-08-04-landmark-relief.md))
ended by requiring one thing before any gate could be written: a deliberately
darkened or infrared-absorbing chin, printed or physically occluded, captured
and refused. This runs it, with household material over the existing vinyl
banner. Same camera and subject as that report, same afternoon.

Two corpora ship beside this file. `2026-08-04-occluder.jsonl` carries the
per-frame region means (59 detected frames) and `2026-08-04-occluder-gate.jsonl`
the shipped gate's own verdicts and cues (24 presentations, including the
same-day genuine baseline). Every number below is regenerated by:

```sh
python3 scripts/research/check-occluder-report.py \
  docs/pad-results/2026-08-04-occluder.jsonl \
  docs/pad-results/2026-08-04-occluder-gate.jsonl \
  --check docs/pad-results/2026-08-04-occluder.md
```

## Material decides the direction, and colour does not predict it

Camera brightness at the chin landmarks, medians. These are readings from one
camera at one geometry, not reflectance measurements: no reference surface was
in frame, and surface angle, distance and coating all contribute.

| chin covering | chin brightness (median) | vs bare print |
|---|---|---|
| none (bare print) | 67.9 | — |
| black polyester cloth | 143.4 | 2.11x brighter |
| black cotton sock | 25.2 | 0.37x |

The polyester read **brighter** than the bare print under the emitter, and
brighter than the forehead (chin/forehead 2.06 against the bare print's 0.692),
which no face in the relief corpus produces. Whatever the material's spectral
properties, as an attack it is worse than no occluder at all: it moved the
relief ratio away from the genuine range.

The cotton read far darker, with nine of its nineteen detected frames at exactly
0 in the chin region.

## It defeats the shipped centre/edge cue

The gate's own readings, from `2026-08-04-occluder-gate.jsonl`:

| presentation | centre/edge ratio | accepted |
|---|---|---|
| bare print | 1.08-1.15 | 6/6 |
| print + polyester | 0.99-1.11 | 4/6 |
| print + cotton | 1.31-1.53 | 6/6 |
| genuine face, same day, same camera | 1.44-1.54 | 8/8 |

The genuine row is the eight-presentation baseline captured the same morning,
committed in the gate corpus beside this file. The larger genuine record is the
[2026-08-02 centre/edge corpus](2026-08-02-center-edge-corpus.jsonl), 60
presentations spanning **1.26 to 1.49**, and that is the range a floor would
have to accept.

The cotton attack reached **1.5345**, above every genuine value in that 60-sample
corpus and inside the same-day baseline. So no floor that accepts the committed
genuine population rejects this cotton presentation. That closes the
raise-the-floor direction harder than the tilt result did: the tilt reached the
edge of the genuine band, a print plus a household item reaches past it, while
flat and square.

## Against the relief cue: the implementation decides

Face bands from the relief corpus are cheek/chin 2.580-3.532 and forehead/chin
3.910-6.266. Of the 19 cotton-occluded frames:

| cue | floor gate (>= face minimum) | band gate (inside the face range) |
|---|---|---|
| cheek/chin | accepts 11 of 19 | accepts 1 of 19 |
| forehead/chin | accepts 9 of 19 | accepts 0 of 19 |

The nine chin-at-zero frames are why the two columns differ so much: a fully
absorbed chin gives an unbounded ratio, which a floor reads as maximally
face-like and a band rejects. **A floor implementation of this cue is defeated
by a sock.** Two rules follow for any future gate: the cue is a band, never a
floor, and a chin region at zero is not measurable and must refuse rather than
score.

## Why the band result is not yet a survival claim

Ten of the nineteen cotton frames have a finite ratio; the other nine have a
zero denominator and are not ratio measurements at all. The ten are 1.312,
1.358, 1.543, 1.555, 1.625, 1.798, 2.382, 2.503, 2.648 and 3.692, and **one of
them falls inside the 2.580-3.532 face band**.

That single in-band sample is enough to show this candidate band did not reject
every presentation tested here. It is not enough to show an attacker can aim.
Coverage, placement and fabric thickness were never recorded, so nothing here
establishes that varying coverage produced the spread, or that the relationship
is continuous or tunable; the early cotton frames also show large left/right
cheek disagreement, which pose or cloth intrusion elsewhere could equally
explain. A controlled sweep with coverage as a measured parameter is the
experiment that would settle it, and it has not been run.

`forehead/chin` was the more robust of the two here, reaching a maximum of 3.35
against a face minimum of 3.910, so no occluded frame entered its band. That is
one material at one placement and is not a margin to build on.

## The trained cue is unaffected by the occluder

The measurements above were taken with the third-party PAD model disabled, so
they describe the native gate alone. Enabling it and repeating both
presentations through the daemon's real authentication path
(`2026-08-04-flir-vs-occluder.jsonl`, six runs, all with 0.0% clipping):

| presentation | native verdict | centre/edge | flir p_fake |
|---|---|---|---|
| bare print | Live | 1.06, 1.06 | 0.988, 1.000 |
| print + cotton over the chin | Live | 1.32, 1.42, 1.44, 1.33 | 0.999, 0.999, 0.998, 0.999 |

The occluder does what the earlier sections describe: it carries the native
centre/edge cue from 1.06 into 1.32-1.44, inside the genuine population of the
[2026-08-02 corpus](2026-08-02-center-edge-corpus.jsonl) (1.26-1.49), and the
native gate returns Live on every one. The trained cue is unmoved, denying at
0.998-0.999 against the bare print's 0.988-1.000.

So the enhancement that defeats every algorithmic cue measured here leaves the
opt-in model's margin intact. Two limits on reading that as a general result:
every frame above was unclipped, and the model's known failure mode is exactly
clipping, where p_fake was measured falling to 0.749 at 24.8% of the face at the
ceiling (#237); and this is one model, one print instrument and one subject.

## What this leaves

- The relief cue still separates an **unmodified** print by a wide margin, and
  that separation survived tilt, curvature, distance and matched exposure. It is
  not print-resistant against an attacker who adds an absorber.
- Untested still: a **controlled coverage sweep**, the experiment that would show
  whether the band is reliably reachable rather than reached once; other fabrics;
  a printed carbon-black chin; and the strobe differential.
- The strobe differential deserves its own note, because it was attempted and
  could not be run. The idea is that an occluder's darkness sits in the material
  and a geometric shadow's in the emitter geometry, so the emitter-off frame
  should tell them apart. In every capture so far the subject receives almost no
  ambient infrared: in the genuine burst the emitter-off frame reads the cheek at
  8-16 and the chin at 1-3 counts, so any ratio from it is quantization noise,
  and the bright part of those frames is background rather than light on the
  subject. Testing it needs an infrared-rich source, daylight or an incandescent
  or halogen lamp, falling on the subject and the print alike.
- One subject, one print, one camera, one room.
