# buffalo_l recognizer: measured basis for the catalog entry

Measurement record for the `buffalo` catalog entry, produced under the
split-source protocol recorded on issue #276: the false-accept side from
public datasets replayed through irlume's own pipeline, the false-reject side
from live captures on this project's cameras, and the threshold set from where
the two populations were observed, never from the publisher's numbers.

## Artifact

`w600k_r50.onnx`, sha256
`4c06341c33c2ca1f86781dab0e829f88ad5b64be9fba56e56bc9ebdefc619e43`, extracted
from the official InsightFace v0.7 `buffalo_l.zip` (zip sha256
`80ffe37d8a5940d59a7384c201a2a38d4741f2f3c51eef46ebb28218a7b0ca2f`), which was
downloaded fresh from the publisher's GitHub release on 2026-08-04 and matched
the measurement copy byte for byte. License: the InsightFace model zoo states
"ALL models are available for non-commercial research purposes only". Training
data: WebFace600K, cleaned from WebFace260M, which was assembled by scraping
260M faces via Google image search from Freebase/IMDB name lists with no
subject consent. Both facts fail ADR-0001's shipping criteria, which is why
this entry is bring-your-own: irlume measures and pins it and never
distributes it.

## Instrument validation

Every leg ran with an AuraFace control through the identical pipeline
(YuNet → align → embed, irlume b8313f4):

- All seven FairFace per-group FAR@0.50 figures reproduced
  [FAIRNESS.md](../FAIRNESS.md) to the printed digit.
- LFW genuine mean 0.584 and EER within 0.05% of the documented 4.17%.
- Two machines ran the LFW leg independently and printed identical summary
  lines.
- Both live-capture sessions carried an AuraFace anchor run that landed on the
  documented live floor (documented min 0.71 / mean 0.85; measured 0.692/0.858
  and 0.762/0.849).

## False-accept side (offline, population-scale)

**LFW**, identity-grouped, full set (13,233 images, 87.3M impostor pairs):
FAR 4e-5 at 0.55; EER 3.9% (AuraFace control: 4.2%).

**FairFace** per-group impostor FAR at the 0.55 operating point, worst first:

| Group | buffalo_l @0.55 | AuraFace @0.55 (control) |
|---|---|---|
| Middle Eastern | 4.02e-4 | 3.40e-4 |
| East Asian | 3.63e-4 | 4.17e-4 |
| Latino/Hispanic | 1.98e-4 | 1.74e-4 |
| Southeast Asian | 1.74e-4 | 2.39e-4 |
| Black | 1.23e-4 | 1.40e-4 |
| White | 1.06e-4 | 6.8e-5 |
| Indian | 1.04e-4 | 9.5e-5 |

The demographic spread narrows from AuraFace's 10x to about 4.5x, and the
worst group's FAR is roughly half of AuraFace's worst — but the worst-served
group SHIFTS: Middle Eastern reads worse under buffalo_l (5.63e-4 @0.50) than
under AuraFace (4.50e-4 @0.50). Fairer overall, not uniformly better, and a
Middle Eastern user choosing this model should know they are the group it
serves worst. The single threshold bounding every group at FMR 1e-4 would be
0.700, in the genuine range; the policy is the same as
[FAIRNESS.md](../FAIRNESS.md)'s for the shipped model: a fixed operating
point, the residual published, the password fallback bounding it.

**SFHQ** synthetic (part 1 from Kaggle
`selfishgene/synthetic-faces-high-quality-sfhq-part-1`, `images/images`,
lexicographic sort, first 15,000 images, 112.5M pairs): FAR 5.7e-5 at 0.55.
The subset is named because the original AuraFace SFHQ figure could not be
reproduced for lack of one, and the protocol now requires it.

## False-reject side (live, this project's cameras, one subject, glasses on)

`calcapture` sessions (production TTA embedding path, artifact pin verified
per record), pairwise genuine cosines:

| Session | min | mean |
|---|---|---|
| Zenbook built-in, desk light | 0.744 | 0.886 |
| Zenbook built-in, side lamp | 0.685 | 0.885 |
| Logitech BRIO (archhost), desk light | 0.793 | 0.863 |

Production-shaped check (probe best-of-25 against a desk pseudo-enrollment):
side-lamp logins clear 0.55 at 25/25 frames but 0.60 at only 24/25 with a
minimum of 0.553. In full darkness the RGB detector found no face in 25/25
frames — that regime is dark IR login, which is disabled for third-party
recognizers because no IR-side measurement exists for them; the practical
answer there is the password. Under screen-glow only (face brightness 18–27),
9/25 frames produced an RGB detection and 5 of those 9 cleared 0.55; the rest
abstained at detection, which denies safely.

## Threshold: 0.55

- At 0.55 the worst FairFace group (4.02e-4) matches the shipped stack's own
  worst group at its operating point (AuraFace @0.55: 4.17e-4): FAR parity
  with what irlume already ships.
- 0.60 was rejected because its cross-condition margin is zero: the side-lamp
  session's production-shaped minimum was 0.553.
- The live floors clear 0.55 by 0.13–0.24 across both cameras. The model's
  accuracy advantage lands as false-reject headroom, the same philosophy the
  shipped 0.55 embodies.
- Template-count scaling applies on top, unchanged.

## Scope

One subject, glasses on, two cameras (Zenbook built-in, Logitech BRIO), one
night of live capture. The IR side is unmeasured, so IR matching, fusion, and
dark login are disabled under this model by the stage-4 wiring. All impostor
mass is web imagery; the live-camera impostor distribution is unmeasurable at
population scale with one subject, the same accepted limitation the shipped
threshold carries, which is why the threshold rule is comparative. Raw
outputs and the working record: `~/irlume-research/2026-08-05-recognition-threshold/`
on the project machines.
