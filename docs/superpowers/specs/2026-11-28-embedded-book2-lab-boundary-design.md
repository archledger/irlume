# Book 2 supervised capture: ownership and stop evidence

**Status:** Proposed for review. This is a non-granting test-backend design, not an approved test or a feature activation. It depends on acceptance of [the G0 class proposal](../../architecture/adr/0042-embedded-book2-rgb-ir-class.md) and separate approval of the exact backend design, the lab environment and any attended device operation. [Stage A](2026-11-27-embedded-book2-experimental-design.md#1-bound-the-first-engineering-slice) established only a pinned interface witness and synthetic behavior. Lab receipt types must not be consumable by enrollment, PAM, matcher or keyring code.

## Approach and trust boundary

Choose a dedicated, attended lab boot with one trusted supervisor and one graph reservation. Use pinned libcamera for front OV5693 RGB and native CIO2 MPLANE for OV7251 `ip3y` on sequential phases. No person, desktop login, real account, playback bridge, network camera, or credential input. Treat the exact lab kernel and its monitor, supervisor, pinned adapter and trusted early boot as part of the test system. This is not isolation from a malicious kernel or controlling privileged operator. Do not use the reporter's normal desktop, repurpose their services, or infer later product support from these results.

Alternatives rejected for the first proof:

- **Stock shared desktop:** advisory lock and local process observations miss retained handles and start before ownership. Stock callers cannot prove driver stop.
- **Single coordinated upstream pipeline:** potentially reduces graph transitions and simplifies lifecycle, but does not fence unrelated callers or establish independent optical OFF. Switch to this research lane if the split cannot pass the ownership, teardown or timing gates; do not weaken those gates.

## 1. Reserve the graph before discovery

Use trusted early userspace in a dedicated test boot. Prove this order: boot with camera-device opens denied and no general worker, confirm deny and clean holder baseline, enumerate passive identities, reserve both graphs, install the exact scoped grant for the supervised worker, then permit any device-handle read. Initial deny must cover future/unknown camera nodes before their numbers are inventoried. Make that transition an explicit test-image acceptance criterion, not a service to run after login. Keep the supervisor outside untrusted worker control; only its reviewed launch chain may allow a worker into the narrow allowed group. Place every other userspace process under effective deny for the full device set. Do not put a blanket deny on an ancestor that would also deny the intended worker; verify the effective hierarchy and device tuples, not just the configuration. The supervisor must establish that no camera-capable process existed before the policy or retained references from a prior attempt. Refuse if the boot sequence, effective membership or initial descriptor baseline is not proved.

Use the [cgroup v2 device controller](https://github.com/gregkh/linux/blob/988117cc1bb17338c586a4e21ade88b5397eac33/Documentation/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.rst#L2788-L2809) for effective device type and major/minor access, including aliases and new opens. It cannot revoke old references. Combine it with separate worker credentials, a scoped device/mount view, no ability to change enforcement, and a private lifecycle that blocks access to another worker's process or descriptors and prohibits exporting camera handles. Check inherited descriptors before each worker starts. Do not expose its proc or IPC access to a peer that could access them. Account for every owned descriptor, mapping, exported buffer and worker until release. An empty process list, a device-cgroup teardown, an advisory lock, or a polling scan is not proof.

Bind the reservation to a single authorized boot, exact loaded stack, current enumerated topology, both CIO2 and ImgU graphs and every endpoint discovery can affect. Get the passive inventory first under the trusted supervisor, with no libcamera manager. Any topology read that needs a device handle must occur only after the outer exclusion is established and unresolved attempt recorded, with that handle covered by the reservation. Device permission alone binds a number, not an object. Before granting a worker open, the lab design must either pin the registered object/generation through open and use with an enforceable lifetime interlock, or prove the controlled boot cannot replace it during the reservation. A later sysfs read or hotplug notice alone leaves a race. If neither can be enforced, do not start discovery.

[Stock IPU3 matching](https://github.com/libcamera-org/libcamera/blob/df8e348d64b4932a0676256ed24f0da3ee9438bc/src/libcamera/pipeline/ipu3/ipu3.cpp#L848-L898) resets links before acquire, then [registration](https://github.com/libcamera-org/libcamera/blob/df8e348d64b4932a0676256ed24f0da3ee9438bc/src/libcamera/pipeline/ipu3/ipu3.cpp#L1040-L1079) initializes both ImgUs and can probe sibling sensors before selection. Reserve the complete affected graph, including sibling ports, inactive links and non-streaming nodes. Reserve does not mean grant use. The reviewed lab handler must check the bound graph and exact permitted effects **before** `disableLinks` or initialization: allow only explicitly listed link changes and resources for the bound front role, not global resets or speculative rear/IR sensor probing. No ImgU index is accepted by convention. If front-only discovery cannot be bounded at these entry points, stop and revise the handler or route. No manager or discovery thread survives into direct IR.

For the first lab design, allow only the pinned **threaded IPA route**, with exact binary/handler/IPA identities and a verified signature. The [pinned factory](https://github.com/libcamera-org/libcamera/blob/df8e348d64b4932a0676256ed24f0da3ee9438bc/include/libcamera/internal/ipa_manager.h#L34-L55) otherwise constructs an isolated proxy. The guard belongs at that branch before proxy construction, not in the caller after the factory returns. Refuse if the threaded predicate fails; do not launch and then inspect the proxy. A signature file or successful build does not prove the loaded branch. A different IPA route needs a separate reviewed descriptor and worker contract. Clear environment overrides and record the selected route without retaining sensor images or authentication inputs.

## 2. Collect stop evidence at the source

Use structured observations from a separately reviewed lab stack. The observer collects kernel and libcamera facts through an isolated, bounded channel; a capture worker may report frame and API outcomes but cannot mint downstream or optical receipts. Start by prototyping private instrumented libcamera plus version-matched kernel/backport observations. A dedicated kernel event instance is a candidate for delivery only if the producer can bind records to the exact operation and the observer can detect loss. Stock queue events and grep for warning strings are not the protocol. For a trace-backed channel, prove buffer lifetime, bounded drain, sequence/epoch coverage and non-loss; sample per-CPU [overrun, commit overrun and drops](https://github.com/gregkh/linux/blob/988117cc1bb17338c586a4e21ade88b5397eac33/Documentation/trace/ftrace.rst#L791-L825) over the whole attempt, refuse on loss, ambiguous identity, unaccounted producer or unavailable counters. Do not let a worker write trace records or reconfigure the observer. Reject a tracing design that cannot positively distinguish not reached, failed and missing.

The supervisor binds each receipt to boot and loaded stack, graph/interface identities, operation and reservation instance, exact phase/direction, worker or driver source, request/queue generation, stop attempt, and a monotonic sequence within the producer's current epoch. A subsystem with asynchronous completion must supply its own operation or queue context, not merely a timestamp or last PID. Reuse, duplicate, missing or contradictory receipts are not valid success. If the instrumentation cannot correlate a completion without guessing, reject the observer design and define a narrower trusted source interface.

First producer seam: in the reviewed lab CIO2 driver, record entry into the receiver disable and DMA stages, and emit distinct outcomes at their actual edges. In [the pinned driver](https://github.com/gregkh/linux/blob/988117cc1bb17338c586a4e21ade88b5397eac33/drivers/media/pci/intel/ipu3/ipu3-cio2.c#L523-L551), the receiver writes establish a request, not that the receiver stopped. The successful poll of `CIO2_CDMAC0_DMA_HALTED` can attest DMA halt for that stop only; timeout emits a failure. A positive receiver-disabled result needs its own reviewed readback semantics. Emit a terminal stage bitmap/disposition from the producer after the relevant path is closed, so an explicit `not reached` on a stage does not rely on missing events. If the sensor start was attempted and errored, skipped sensor stop is not proof of standby; require separate authoritative standby evidence or hold. In the disposable guest test the real producer seam with controlled acknowledgement, timeout and skipped-stage inputs, as well as testing consumer refusal on omitted events. Zero ring overruns cannot make a producer that never emitted pass.

| Required observation | Earliest trustworthy source and check |
| --- | --- |
| Before first effect | Establish owner and passive baseline, persist unresolved intent and revalidate both before any possible effect. Record start-effect boundaries from handle access, discovery/configuration and start, not just `STREAMON` success. |
| RGB software | Track accepted requests and completion/cancellation, all consumers and buffer references. Confirm allocator release, camera teardown, manager thread exit and no eligible stale discovery. Public stop and a quiet callback are not receipts for lower layers. |
| RGB partial start and teardown | Record actual startup stage and rollback outcomes at the [IPU3 start/error/stop boundary](https://github.com/libcamera-org/libcamera/blob/df8e348d64b4932a0676256ed24f0da3ee9438bc/src/libcamera/pipeline/ipu3/ipu3.cpp#L721-L784). Independently observe each active ImgU queue/firmware stop, idle/termination, halt and power-down obligations. The [kernel helpers](https://github.com/gregkh/linux/blob/988117cc1bb17338c586a4e21ade88b5397eac33/drivers/staging/media/ipu3/ipu3-css.c#L608-L647) can log a failure without an application-level stop error. A second stop after failed start is not a substitute. |
| CIO2 and OV7251 end state | Observe which CSI-2 receiver and CIO2 DMA stages actually ran, receiver-off and applicable DMA halt acknowledgement, and sensor standby outcome with verified transport/state semantics. In [CIO2 start/rollback and stop](https://github.com/gregkh/linux/blob/988117cc1bb17338c586a4e21ade88b5397eac33/drivers/media/pci/intel/ipu3/ipu3-cio2.c#L1002-L1060), failed sensor start and normal stop have different paths; [vb2](https://github.com/gregkh/linux/blob/988117cc1bb17338c586a4e21ade88b5397eac33/drivers/media/common/videobuf2/videobuf2-core.c#L1769-L1789) clears its started flag after failure. In the [published OV7251 writer](https://github.com/antoine79/surface-ir-camera/blob/0126416f472674c47f652f4062ca4e69db4e70a8/kernel/backport/ov7251.c#L711-L728), a nonnegative transport result alone does not prove the requested write. The exact loaded module must be reviewed separately. |
| IR software and optical OFF | Verify queue state, buffers, mappings and device references released as appropriate. Independently measure actual emission and OFF for the exact reservation and exposure window. A dark frame, cached value, receiver log, or clean queue release never counts as optical evidence. |
| Handover and final release | Match expected exact graph/link state while still owning both media graphs. Restore only under the active reservation; verify final state and release once. If authority is lost, do not restore another peer's graph or start a later phase. |

An attempted start, configuration or link reset can change hardware even if it returned an error. Treat missing proof as unknown. A positive `not reached` result must come from an independent observer of that precise stage and cannot replace owner, graph or optical checks. A clean, bounded refusal can finish restoration and release but may not continue the schedule within that attempt. On unknown stop, owner/release, worker death, observer loss or optical state, the sole outcome is a hold.

## 3. Keep recovery and optical qualification separate

Create durable unresolved intent before discovery or any side effect on the controlled test storage. When an attempt is unresolved, disabled, restarted or its storage provenance is uncertain, refuse new work. Do not make a clean marker by catching a timeout, restarting the worker or turning off the class. A later maintainer-reviewed recovery operation needs fresh, exact-operation authority, graph re-acquisition and independently observed safe terminal state, none of which is established by reboot alone. A filesystem-only journal is not a coherent rollback witness; do not claim rollback-safe reactivation without a separately reviewed mechanism. No existing profile, ordinary route, credential or production hold mechanism is touched by this lab.

**A hold cannot stop active emission.** Start with an offline slice. A later non-arming device stage is allowed only if a reviewed independent barrier proves emission cannot occur during that exact sequence; otherwise even a purported non-arming capture is out. The [published OV7251 start](https://github.com/antoine79/surface-ir-camera/blob/0126416f472674c47f652f4062ca4e69db4e70a8/kernel/backport/ov7251.c#L1378-L1410) reapplies cached controls before streaming. A software `off` setting is not proof of a non-arming route.

Before any armed trial, require a separately reviewed means to end emission within a preapproved bound even if the worker, observer or kernel capture path stalls. The limit and physical stop must be independent of the software path being tested and remain effective on supervision loss. No suitable cutoff is verified today; without it do not arm. The attended protocol must calibrate the independent detector with a known check source, establish the live OFF baseline at the exact affected emitter, capture the relevant pulse profile without lost monitoring, and verify OFF continuously through restoration and release. Loss of optical observation means trigger the reviewed end-of-emission control, retain a hold and never infer OFF. Choose thresholds, time limits and stop conditions from separately reviewed measurement design, not from nominal controls.

B12 is G3q, not a production grant or a lab bypass of G1. Pretrial G1 reviews the instrumented stack, expected graph/role contract, permitted operations and independent bounded safety prerequisites. G3q produces the actual timing, stop and optical observations for later separate G2 review; those observations are not prerequisites already claimed by G1. Require an approved exact boot, consent, limits and recovery.

One single-use G3q scope may explicitly enumerate both IR bursts in a named `IR → RGB → IR` attempt under one reservation. Each other order or standalone route is a separate attempt. Within the named attempt, every potentially emitting operation, including a start that can restore cached strobe state, needs a fresh recheck of that scope and reservation, current independent safety and initial-state checks, and its own durable [per-arming record](../../architecture/adr/0041-admission-first-embedded-ir.md#10-complete-safe-teardown-before-returning-a-final-embedded-outcome) distinct from the original attempt intent. Do not reuse a pre-arm clearance or receipt, add an unlisted burst, or treat the second listed burst as automatically cleared. Confirm matching closure, teardown and independent OFF before another arm, handover or release.

Use a non-person target. Verify each route alone and then both `RGB → IR → RGB` and `IR → RGB → IR` when their exact phase scopes are authorized. Measure actual delivered payload and frame completeness against receiver evidence, timing and clocks. Stop on unknown observer, unsupported scope, unexpected graph/emitter state or incomplete restoration, with pre-agreed recovery. G3q demonstrates only transport/optical behavior on the tested boot and may inform later G2 review; it authorizes neither G2 nor any future grant. Later unattended optical inference and G7/G8 need their own evidence.

## 4. Offline acceptance and separate device gate

Plan the first offline backend and observer slice in an isolated disposable guest with its own boot, no physical-device passthrough, and only dummy endpoints. Use its actual guest kernel and cgroup policy for B01 and B02; the Stage A namespace and synthetic model alone do not exercise early-boot enforcement. Approval of this draft does not authorize booting or configuring the guest; that belongs to the reviewed implementation plan and scope. Use controlled hardware inputs for producer tests and injected receipts for consumer tests. B01 through B11 must pass before proposing exact-unit activity. B12 is separately approved G3q. These are unrun requirements:

| ID | Test and required result |
| --- | --- |
| B01 | Prove enforcement and trusted launch membership before any discovery callback. Missing boot/policy evidence or a holder from an earlier stage produces no mutation. |
| B02 | In the actual dummy guest boot, probe allowed devices and denied aliases/peers, including inherited/transferred references. If using an identity interlock, replace a dummy endpoint behind the same device number and require refusal before open/discovery. If using a non-replacement policy, show an in-scope replacement attempt is prevented rather than bypassing that policy. Neither result qualifies the Book 2. |
| B03 | Reject unexpected graph, unlisted discovery effect, hotplug/rebind/generation change and proxy branch mismatch before that effect. No second backend starts after ownership becomes unknown. |
| B04 | For each order run a separate single-use G3q fixture operation, one reservation and exact phase generations. In `IR → RGB → IR`, permit two explicitly listed bursts only with fresh independent per-arm clearance; wrong stack, boot, endpoint, queue/stop ID, sequence, duplicate or foreign source refuses. |
| B05 | At each discovery/config/start boundary that can have effects, supply confirmed or missing rollback independently. Positive bounded denial may release; missing or unconfirmed result keeps the hold. |
| B06 | Exercise the actual producer seam with controlled DMA acknowledge/timeout, a receiver-disable request without readback, failed sensor start without standby proof, and explicit skipped-stage disposition. Use additional fixtures for ImgU firmware/rollback and misleading `STREAMOFF=0`. No next phase from aggregate success. |
| B07 | Detect observer drop, sideband overflow/mismatch, missing producer event with zero overruns, worker and supervisor loss, and reader restart. Verify exact scoped evidence is required after interruption. |
| B08 | Owner lost means no restore; restore mismatch means no release; release proof missing means no completed outcome. |
| B09 | Inject absent, stale or unrelated optical receipt, lack of independently bounded cutoff, unlisted burst and unsynced/duplicate per-arming record (including reuse on the second named burst). No arming or handover from software success alone. No measurement claims from injected receipts. |
| B10 | Durably leave an unresolved attempt across process exit and follow-up start. Failing storage or known restore must hold rather than admit. No reset command in the first slice. |
| B11 | Assert lab binaries, typed receipts and fixtures have no product admission or credential matching consumer, and ordinary paths remain byte-for-byte untouched by the slice. |
| B12 | Separately approved G3q after reviewed instrumented G1. Repeat early ownership and end-to-end stop/optics controls under the exact Book 2 boot. No emitting operation without independently bounded end-of-emission and calibrated measurement. If any key proof cannot be enforced or measured, retain a non-arming/offline restriction or stop. |

Do not claim an outside review has validated an instrumented path merely by reviewing this draft. Follow the approved split-first direction only if these gates remain satisfiable; otherwise report the failed boundary rather than inventing a timeout or a simpler approval proxy.
